Common questions

Is my card information safe?

Yes. You enter payment details in Paystack's secure window. This page never sees or stores your card number.

What if I get charged but see no confirmation?

Keep your payment reference and contact us with it. We can confirm the payment from our records.

Can I choose my own amount?

Yes. Tap one of the amounts or type any amount you like.
